RAYE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

RAYE - Habit Tracker ("RAYE") is developed by Doucan Yein. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data we collect

RAYE stores habit, expense, and app settings data locally on your device. If you enable Apple Health integration, RAYE reads the health metrics you authorize (such as steps, workouts, sleep, or water) only to complete linked habits.

Data we do not collect

We do not operate a user account server for core app data. We do not sell your personal information.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.
